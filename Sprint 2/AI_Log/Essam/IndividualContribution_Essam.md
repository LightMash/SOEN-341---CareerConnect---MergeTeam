## Sprint #2

<p align="justify">
For this sprint, I was responsible for the recruiter side of the frontend for two user stories: job posting management (US-04) and application status updates (US-07). For US-04 (Task 4.3), I built the recruiter's job postings screen, where a recruiter can create, edit, close and reopen their postings. The form checks the same limits as the backend, like the description length and the salary range, and shows clear error messages when something is wrong. Open postings are listed first. I connected this screen to the job posting API built by a teammate and made the dashboard show it to recruiters instead of the job seeker view. For US-07 (Task 7.2), I added an Applicants panel to each posting that lists everyone who applied, with their name, email, resume and date. The recruiter can change each application's status (Applied, Under Review, Interview, Offered, Rejected), and the change is saved right away. The panel also shows each application's status history, which updates after every change. I tested both features against the real backend before opening my pull requests, resolved a merge conflict with main on my first pull request, and documented my AI usage in my Sprint 2 AI log.
